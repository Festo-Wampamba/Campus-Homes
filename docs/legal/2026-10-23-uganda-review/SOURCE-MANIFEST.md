## External sources and use

- [ICT Acts and Laws index](https://ict.go.ug/resources/documents/acts-and-laws). Opened; contains the four listed Acts. This index alone does not establish that its original PDFs include all amendments.
- [ICT Policies and Regulations index](https://ict.go.ug/resources/documents/policies-and-regulations). Opened and screened; its policy categories are not treated as equivalent to binding Acts.
- [ICT Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019](https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/Data-Protection-and-Privacy-Act-2019.pdf). Official original scan, also provided locally.
- [NITA-U Cap 97 text](https://nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-09/Data%20Protection%20and%20Privacy%20Act%20cap%2097.pdf). Official source used for section checks; cross-reference typo and version caveats appear in the privacy ledger.
- [PDPO privacy Regulations 2021](https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2022/03/Data_Protection_and_Privacy_Regulations-2021.pdf) and [official NITA-U archived scan](https://oldsite.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2022-11/Data_Protection_and_Privacy_Regulations-2021.pdf). The older PDPO URL can serve a shell; the NITA-U scan was extracted/OCR checked for relevant provisions.
- [PDPO regulator portal](https://pdpo.go.ug/) and [knowledge centre](https://pdpo.go.ug/knowledge-center). Current forms and register routes; the portal depends on JavaScript. No Akolet certificate was verified.
- [PDPO registration guidance](https://pdpo.go.ug/media//2022/01/20102021105143-Registration_Classification_and_Guidance_Notes.pdf) and [registration form](https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2022/01/31122021140148-Form_2_-_Application_for_Registration_Renewal_of_Registration.pdf). Indexed official material; historic URLs may require the current portal to retrieve the file.
- [Privacy Act searchable consolidation](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2019/9/eng%402023-12-31) and [Regulations consolidation](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/si/2021/21/eng%402021-03-12). Searchable reproductions used with official text, subject to point-in-time and access limitations.
- [ICT Electronic Transactions Act 2011](https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/UGANDA-Electronic-Transactions-Act-No.-8-of-2011.pdf). Official PDF opened and downloaded copy extracted; sections 3, 5–9, 13–14, 24–32 and Schedule 2 are material.
- [ICT Electronic Transactions Regulations 2013](https://ict.go.ug/public/site/documents/Electronic-Transction-Regulations-SI-42-of-2013.pdf). Official SI 42 PDF opened, particularly regulations 3–12 and hosting/reporting provisions.
- [ICT Electronic Signatures Act 2011](https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/UGANDA-Electronic-Signatures-Act-No.-7-of-2011.pdf). Official PDF opened and local copy extracted; reliability conditions do not make every click an advanced signature.
- [Contracts Act 2010 searchable text](https://ulii.org/akn/ug/act/2010/7/~part_IV__sec_28) and [PDF](https://media.ulii.org/media/legislation/111539/source_file/34188fb82877087f/2010-7.pdf). Indexed statutory text used for sections 9–12; direct fetches were access-restricted on some attempts.
- [Parliament Constitution page](https://www.parliament.go.ug/page/constitution) and [searchable Constitution](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/statute/1995/constitution/eng%402023-12-31). Constitutional framework references; not an opinion on a particular constitutional claim.
- [Children Act updated record](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/statute/1996/6/eng%402024-08-16) and [earlier PDF](https://media.ulii.org/media/legislation/18231/source_file/1996-6.pdf). Age and guardian context; do not mistake the service rule for a universal statutory contracting ban.
- [Parliament Landlord and Tenant Act PDF](https://bills.parliament.ug/attachments/The%20Landlord%20and%20Tenant%20Act%2C%202022.pdf), [Parliament Acts 2022 index](https://www.parliament.go.ug/acts/acts-2022), [ULII consolidation](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2022/9/eng%402023-12-31) and [ULII PDF](https://media.ulii.org/media/legislation/122651/source_file/6cb1b80d316d5252/2022-9.pdf). Primary-law reproductions used in agreement review; current amendment/Gazette confirmation remains necessary. Parliament PDF exceeded the web tool size limit on the parent's fetch.
- [Computer Misuse Act original](https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/UGANDA-Computer-Misuse-Act-No.-2-of-2011-1.pdf). Local copy examined; not relied on as a current complete cybercrime code.
- [Alternative Digitalk judgment record 2026 UGCC 3](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/judgment/ugcc/2026/3/eng%402026-03-17). Primary judgment record and indexed holding; full page fetch restricted. [UBC report on the Attorney General response](https://ubc.go.ug/2026/03/26/govt-drops-appeal-on-computer-misuse-act-ruling-opts-to-return-law-to-parliament/) is corroborative reporting, not the judgment or proof of no subsequent enactment.
- [Copyright Act current searchable record](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2006/19/eng%402026-05-22). Indexed record identifies Act 8 of 2026 amendment; do not rely exclusively on a 2006 original for current IP advice.
- [Bank of Uganda payments framework](https://bou.or.ug/financial_infrastructure_innovation) and [NPS licensing FAQ](https://bou.or.ug/uploads/Frequently_asked_Questions_on_the_NPS_ACT_2020_and_the_NPS_Regulations_2021_5818aec7f5.pdf). Conditional payment-role assessment, not evidence that Akolet holds a licence.
- [Trade Ministry consumer-law statement](https://www.mtic.go.ug/uganda-partners-with-comesa-to-fast-track-implementation-of-the-competition-and-consumer-protection-laws/) and [2026 Parliament record](https://cmis.parliament.go.ug/cmis/views/49d72657-45dd-4428-9726-5745dd242880%253B1.0). Distinguish a Bill from commenced law; recheck before publication.
- [DigsConnect privacy](https://www.digsconnect.com/privacy) and [terms](https://www.digsconnect.com/terms-and-conditions). Both opened; structure and business-model comparison only. Some repeat fetches timed out. No South African statutory clause or commercial product was adopted without an independent basis.
- [RentEase Uganda tenancy guide](https://www.renteaseuganda.com/blog/uganda-landlord-tenant-act-2022-guide). Supplied secondary lead opened and checked against statute references in the agreement review.
- [Groq data documentation](https://console.groq.com/docs/your-data) and [privacy policy](https://groq.com/privacy-policy). Official provider material opened. The data page states default inference non-retention with reliability/abuse exceptions, configurable controls and US storage of retained customer data. That is not verification of Akolet's account, contract, countries of access or deletion settings.

The Akolet website and contact page were attempted but were not retrievable through the web tool. Contact values therefore rely on the owner's instructions and current contact-details.ts rather than an independent live verification. The very tall DigsConnect screenshots were a visual reference; legible website text, rather than the downscaled image, was used for subject coverage.
