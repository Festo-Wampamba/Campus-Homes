## 2 What the platform provides

CampusHomes helps students search and compare advertised accommodation, view published inspection information, contact a landlord, request a viewing, make a temporary reservation, request a booking and record related communication and move-in activity. It also provides listing and administration tools for landlords and authorised property teams.

Unless a separate written appointment expressly says otherwise, we do not own or let the advertised accommodation, sign a tenancy for a landlord, set rent, receive rent or deposits, guarantee a tenant or landlord, or carry out an eviction. The landlord and student remain responsible for agreeing and performing their tenancy and for complying with applicable law.

If an agreed service makes Akolet a limited marketing or communications agent, the written appointment must identify the principal and scope and be disclosed as required by law. Describing the service as a platform does not override the legal consequences of what staff actually do. A broader management or payment appointment requires a separate signed agreement and the necessary regulatory assessment.

Keep your sign-in method secure and report suspected compromise promptly. You are responsible for authorised use of your account, but these terms do not automatically make you liable for every unauthorised act regardless of its cause. We remain responsible for our own legal and security duties.

## 6 Free reservations and expiry

A reservation is a free, temporary platform hold of an available bed while you arrange the next steps. CampusHomes takes no reservation payment in the current service. A hold does not itself grant possession, create a tenancy, prove rent payment or guarantee that all tenancy conditions have been agreed.

The server-confirmed expiry shown in the reservation record governs the hold. A countdown on your device is a display aid. The current service permits up to five active reservations, subject to inventory and the limit of one active reservation per hostel room category and bathroom configuration. Before changing those limits, we must update the interface and applicable terms consistently.

You can cancel an eligible hold through My reservations. A hold may expire or be released under the displayed rules, for a corrected inventory conflict or for a necessary safety or fraud response. We should tell you when a hold is released early and explain the next step where appropriate. Release of a platform hold is not an eviction or automatic cancellation of a tenancy that was formed separately. Do not pay someone who describes a CampusHomes free hold as a compulsory CampusHomes fee.

## 7 Booking requests tenancy and move in

After viewing or otherwise assessing the accommodation and agreeing the relevant terms, you can send a booking request. The landlord decides whether to accept and records the appropriate confirmation. An automated receipt or a student pressing a request button is not the landlord's acceptance.

A confirmed status records the landlord's platform action. Whether the parties have also formed a binding tenancy or other agreement depends on their actual offer, acceptance, terms and applicable law; a platform label cannot remove a contract or statutory right already created. The landlord and student must identify each other and provide and retain the tenancy record required by law. An agreement made through an electronic form must satisfy the applicable legal requirements, including any property-registration or document-form requirements.

Pay rent, deposits and lawful landlord charges directly to the identified landlord through the agreed channel and obtain the required receipt. Check the tenancy and payment instructions before sending money. CampusHomes does not hold these funds, act as escrow or promise a refund from funds it has never received. A landlord's recorded payment confirmation is not a CampusHomes bank receipt.

Arrange keys, physical access, room condition and move-in directly with the landlord. A move-in confirmation in CampusHomes records the relevant action; it is not a property inspection, legal waiver or guarantee that every tenancy obligation has been performed. Report material discrepancies promptly and keep your own agreements, receipts and messages.

## 8 Cancellation refunds and statutory rights

Cancelling a free CampusHomes hold carries no CampusHomes cancellation charge. Once a booking or tenancy has been agreed, cancellation, withdrawal and refunds depend on the lawful terms disclosed before acceptance and any mandatory Ugandan rights. A landlord must not substitute a hidden no-refund rule after payment.

The landlord is responsible for lawful refunds of rent, deposits and landlord charges paid directly to it. Where a room is unavailable or materially misdescribed, report it and seek an appropriate remedy from the landlord. We can assist with communication and investigate platform conduct; that does not make us the holder of the payment. Akolet remains responsible for refunds of its own charges where legally or contractually due.

Uganda's Electronic Transactions Act contains consumer disclosure, cancellation, performance and refund provisions. Their applicability and any exceptions depend on the actual transaction. Nothing in these terms waives or reduces a non-excludable right. In particular, we do not impose a universal no-refund rule or claim that every accommodation-related transaction is automatically exempt from electronic-consumer protections. Obtain advice on the particular dispute if the parties disagree.

## 9 Landlord duties and tenancy protections

Landlords must have lawful authority to advertise and let, provide evidence reasonably needed for the listing service, obtain required permissions and disclose material changes promptly. They must keep information and availability accurate, provide habitable accommodation, handle repairs and access lawfully, avoid unlawful discrimination, protect tenant information and apply the agreed terms fairly.

For tenancies to which Uganda's Landlord and Tenant Act 2022 applies, statutory obligations on identification, tenancy copies, receipts, deposits, advance rent, quiet enjoyment, repairs, notices and lawful termination continue to apply. A house rule, website term or platform status cannot exclude a mandatory provision of that Act. The Act has scope exclusions; a residence's student label alone does not determine whether an exclusion applies.

Before agreement, landlords must disclose the full rent and payment period and any requirement to pay a semester in advance. They must record any agreement needed to depart from the statutory advance-rent default, comply with applicable security-deposit limits and give the required receipts. A service or commission fee must not be used to conceal an unlawful rent or deposit charge.

Landlords must not use CampusHomes suspension, a cancelled hold or a review dispute as permission to enter or evict unlawfully, seize belongings or disconnect essential services unlawfully. Any occupancy restriction requires a lawful basis; a platform gender filter does not certify legality. Authorised custodians and agents must act within the scope of their appointment.
