## 1 Who is responsible for your information

CampusHomes is a student accommodation platform operated by Akolet Properties Ltd, referred to here as Akolet, we or us. We decide how information is used to run CampusHomes accounts, listing and inspection services, reservations, communications, support and platform security. For those activities we act as a data controller under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019.

This policy applies to campushomes.co.ug and its student, landlord and staff portals, inspections and related CampusHomes communications. A landlord may independently decide how to use information for a tenancy. Where we provide a landlord with tools to administer tenant information on its instructions, the applicable roles and responsibilities must also be described in the notice on that form and our agreement with the landlord. A landlord's own legal responsibilities do not disappear because it uses CampusHomes.

## 2 Information we collect and where it comes from

We collect information you enter, information another person lawfully supplies for an enquiry or tenancy, observations made during inspections, and technical records generated when you use the service.

- Account and contact information includes your name, username, email address, telephone numbers, sign-in identifiers and verification status. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive the identity information authorised through that sign-in process.
- Student profile information can include university, year of study, date of birth, gender, nationality, address and emergency-contact details. The form must identify required and optional fields and explain the consequence of leaving a required field blank.
- Landlord and property information includes legal and business names, contact details, authority to advertise or let, verification information and documents where requested, property location, room inventory, rent, deposits, charges, amenities, rules, photographs and inspection records.
- Service records include favourites, reservations and their expiry, booking requests and confirmations, move-in confirmations, viewing requests, enquiries, landlord messages, reviews, calendar reminders, complaints and safety reports.
- Tenant registration and agreement forms can include identity or passport numbers, student registration details, course, parent or guardian and next-of-kin contacts, rent and move-in information, a typed or drawn signature, and other information identified on the particular form. Some forms may ask for optional medical conditions or allergies. Section 4 explains the additional protections for these records.
- Support information includes what you send to our team, related replies and references. If you use CampusHomes AI, your question and recent conversation messages are sent to an external AI service as described in section 7.
- Technical and usage information includes page paths, selected action events, search terms and filters recorded by the search service, listing references and result counts, security and error logs, IP addresses, browser information and session records where generated. Our browser page-view event does not include the search query string, but the separate search service currently records submitted search text. Do not put private information into a hostel search.
- Direct payment records may include a landlord's confirmation of an amount received and the payment method or booking reference. CampusHomes does not take rent or deposits in its current service and does not ask for your card PIN, mobile-money PIN or sign-in code.

An identity document, medical detail, signature or private message is not ordinary public listing content. We do not ask you to post it in a review or listing photograph. Tell other people before supplying their contact information, provide only what is necessary, and give them access to this policy. Where another lawful notification or consent is required, we and the relevant landlord must arrange it rather than assuming that you can consent for another adult.

## 3 Why we use information and the legal basis

We use information for specified purposes and only where Ugandan law permits it. Uganda's Act generally requires prior consent, with specific exceptions including processing necessary for a contract or requested steps before a contract and processing required by law. Accepting website terms is not consent to every possible use of information.

- To establish and secure an account, provide requested searches and saved listings, operate a reservation, route a message or viewing request, and record booking activity, we use the information necessary to provide the service you request and perform the relevant contract.
- To check a landlord's identity and authority, assess a listing, record an inspection and investigate inaccuracies, we use information necessary for the listing service and applicable legal duties. We explain the documents required and avoid retaining unnecessary copies.
- To respond to support and safety requests, resolve disputes and preserve relevant evidence, we use information necessary to handle your request, fulfil our obligations or comply with law. A report may need to be shared with the affected person so they can respond, but unnecessary reporter details must be protected.
- For optional health information, optional promotional communications and other processing that relies on consent, we ask for a specific, informed choice. You can withdraw that consent through the privacy contact without making earlier lawful processing unlawful. We explain any practical effect on the optional service.
- For security, fraud prevention and operational measurement, we document the applicable statutory permission, necessity and safeguards. Cookieless measurement is still subject to privacy obligations where the records identify or can be linked to a person. We do not treat a general claim of legitimate interest as permission for unrelated collection.

These general grounds do not displace the additional restrictions for special personal data, including financial information, in section 4. We do not sell personal information. We do not use account information for unrelated partner marketing merely because you search, reserve or contact a landlord. Any new purpose must be assessed and explained before it begins, with a new consent where required.

## 4 Sensitive information and tenant forms

Health conditions, medical records and financial information are special personal data under section 9 of Ugandan law, as are religious or philosophical beliefs, political opinions and sexual-life information. Rent and payment records can contain financial information even when CampusHomes does not receive the payment. These categories need their own assessment and a permitted condition under section 9, in addition to the general processing rules. Do not put them in ordinary searches, public reviews or the AI assistant.

If a tenant form offers an optional medical or allergy field, the form must explain why it is needed, who can see it, its retention period and the applicable legal basis before submission. Where section 9 subsection 3 paragraph b is relied on, the information must be given freely with the data subject's consent; we require a separate affirmative choice for optional health information. Declining it must not prevent use of the ordinary search or reservation service. Section 9 also contains specific employer-duty and qualifying non-profit-body conditions, which are not general permissions for CampusHomes. A general contractual, medical-purpose or legal-obligation ground under section 7 must not be assumed to override section 9. Counsel must confirm the permitted treatment of financial and any emergency medical records before that processing is offered.

Landlords must collect only information reasonably required for the identified tenancy purpose and must not use tenant forms to collect unrelated personal details. A general declaration at the end of a form does not replace a specific notice or necessary consent. Identity documents and signatures require restricted access and must not be placed in a public photo bucket.

## 5 Children and guardians

CampusHomes account and reservation services are intended for adults aged 18 and above. This is the proposed service eligibility rule, not a statement that every Ugandan contract involving a younger person is invalid. Under-18 accounts and reservations are unavailable until we have an approved and supported guardian process. If you are under 18, do not submit personal information or reserve through the ordinary workflow; your parent or legal guardian may contact our team to discuss an appropriate route. Publicly viewing general information does not authorise submission of a child's data.

Before a supported guardian process collects a child's data, we must ascertain age, verify the appropriate adult's authority and obtain prior parental or guardian consent unless a specific legal exception applies. Merely entering a parent's telephone number is not guardian consent. If child information has been submitted without an appropriate basis, contact us so we can restrict further use and decide what must be deleted or lawfully retained. We do not use children's information for targeted marketing.

## 6 Who receives information

Public visitors can see published property descriptions, location information, approved photographs, room features, rules, relevant landlord profile information and public reviews. Landlord join dates and response summaries, where shown, derive from platform records. A response estimate is historical information, not a promise that every message will receive a reply within 24 hours.

The relevant landlord and authorised property team receive information needed for your enquiry, viewing, reservation, booking or tenant administration. They do not receive unrestricted access to all your CampusHomes records. Other participants can retain messages you sent them, and a landlord may need to retain its own lawful tenancy records after your platform account closes.

Authorised Akolet personnel may access records needed for support, inspections, security, moderation, compliance or dispute handling. Platform messages are stored and processed by the service; do not assume they are end-to-end encrypted or inaccessible to authorised support personnel. Content or contact filtering may be used to prevent abuse, subject to appropriate safeguards and review.

Service-provider categories include hosting and database infrastructure, identity and sign-in systems, email and verification delivery, storage, network protection, error monitoring, messaging infrastructure and the optional AI assistant. The source code supports Logto, Google sign-in, Resend, Backblaze B2, legacy Cloudinary storage, Sentry, Soketi and Groq. WhatsApp or Africa's Talking integrations may be used where the relevant verification or notification channel is enabled. Which services are active, their legal roles and processing countries must be confirmed in the published provider register. Code support alone does not prove that a provider is deployed.

We disclose information to authorities where lawfully required, and to professional advisers where necessary and appropriately protected. If a business transfer affects your information, we must assess the lawful basis, confidentiality and notice requirements. We do not treat that possibility as permission for unrelated sale or marketing of user records.

## 7 AI support and external services

CampusHomes AI provides general guidance from the user manual. In the reviewed implementation, your submitted question and up to 12 recent conversation messages are sent through our server to Groq's API to produce a reply. The app limits each message to 1000 characters. It does not deliberately attach your account profile, reservation or private landlord messages to the AI request, but anything you type into the chat is part of the transmitted content.

The chat shown in your browser is held in page memory in the reviewed implementation. That does not mean the external provider or infrastructure keeps no records. Provider retention, access, processing countries and any secondary use must be disclosed and assessed before this feature handles personal information. Do not send identity numbers, medical information, passwords, payment credentials or another person's private details. You can use the user manual or contact our team instead. AI replies may be inaccurate and do not decide your tenancy, booking eligibility or a legal dispute.

OpenStreetMap tile servers receive technical connection information, including your IP address, when a map loads. If you choose a Google directions link, Google processes the resulting visit under its own terms. Clicking a WhatsApp link opens an external service and shares the content you choose to send; the floating shortcut itself is locally served and is not an embedded third-party chat widget. Email, telephone and social services you independently use also have their own privacy practices.

## 8 Processing outside Uganda

Some infrastructure and service providers may process information outside Uganda. Before a transfer, we must identify the destination, recipients and onward transfers and satisfy section 19 of the Act and regulation 30 of the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations 2021. We must demonstrate the required equivalent protection to the Office or obtain the appropriate informed consent, and obtain any express consent required for onward transfer to a third country. Where a destination is not on the relevant Gazette list, the organisation bears the proof burden for adequacy. A provider contract by itself does not prove that the destination meets Ugandan requirements.

Before publication, Akolet must complete its internal recipient and country register and include the verified recipients, destinations and safeguards in the user-facing notice. Publishing a separate provider register is a proposed transparency measure, not a claim that the Act prescribes that exact format. If a feature relies on your consent for an international transfer, we must explain the destination and relevant implications before the transfer. For AI help, the manual and team contact provide alternatives. We do not obtain blanket permission to send data anywhere in the world by your continued use of the website.

## 9 Cookies and storage on your device

CampusHomes uses session and sign-in cookies to authenticate requests and protect the sign-in process. The reviewed application session cookie is campushomes-session-v1, with a seven-day application session lifetime; separate identity-provider and temporary sign-in cookies may have different lifetimes. The deployed cookie inventory must list the actual names, purposes and expiry periods.

Local storage keeps preferences such as your theme, recently viewed listings, sidebar state and dismissal of the cookie notice. Session storage can keep the position of the movable WhatsApp shortcut and navigation state. Staff inspection tools can keep draft photographs, location observations and inspection notes in IndexedDB until synchronised or removed. Those drafts are work records on the staff device and require device safeguards and a cleanup process.

The reviewed browser analytics component sends first-party page-view and selected action events without a tracking cookie. This is separate from search-service events and security logs. We have not verified production scripts or identity-provider cookies through a live cookie audit. Optional advertising, third-party tracking or new persistent identifiers must not be introduced under an essential-only notice; the purpose, legal basis and choice mechanism must be updated first.

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings. This may sign you out, remove preferences or remove unsynchronised drafts; it does not erase records already sent to us. Removing a cookie notice does not withdraw every consent or close your account.

## 10 Retention and deletion

We retain personal information only for its specified purpose, a lawful related function or an applicable legal requirement. Relevant factors include the account or tenancy relationship, verification need, dispute or safety investigation, contractual evidence and statutory record-keeping. A general wish to keep information useful indefinitely is not a retention rule.

The published notice must include the approved schedule for account records, identity documents, tenant forms and health details, messages and support records, inspections, search and usage events, security logs, backups and external-provider copies. Exact periods remain subject to approval and implementation; this draft does not invent statutory periods for these categories.

When retention is no longer justified, the information must be securely deleted, destroyed or de-identified so it cannot be reconstructed in identifiable form. A legal hold must be documented, access restricted and the hold reviewed. Account closure, account deletion, anonymisation and removal from a public listing are different actions. Removing a database row does not automatically remove storage objects, identity-provider records, backups or a landlord's independently held tenancy records.

You may request account closure or deletion through the privacy contact. We will explain any information that must be retained, the reason and applicable period, and which other controller you may need to contact. We must manage deletion across our own systems and contracted providers and keep failed deletion tasks under review. Backup expiry and restoration controls must prevent an erased record from being silently restored into active use.

## 11 Security and incidents

We are responsible for appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, including access restrictions, secure transmission, authentication, private storage for sensitive records, staff confidentiality, logging and regular verification of controls. We also require appropriate safeguards from processors. These are continuing duties and do not amount to a guarantee that no incident can occur.

If we believe personal data has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we must notify Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office through the prescribed Authority procedure immediately and provide information about remedial action. We must follow the Authority's directions on informing affected people. We do not substitute a general 72-hour waiting period for the Ugandan notification requirement.

Protect your sign-in method, avoid shared-device access to sensitive forms, sign out after use, and report suspected misuse through the contact details above. CampusHomes staff should never ask for your password, verification code or mobile-money PIN.

## 12 Your rights and how to make a request

Subject to the conditions and safeguards in Ugandan law, you may request confirmation and access to information about you and its recipients; correction or deletion of records that are inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading, unlawfully obtained or no longer authorised to be retained; prevention of certain harmful or incompatible processing; and an end to direct marketing. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Qualifying significant decisions based solely on automated processing are subject to statutory protections and reconsideration rights. The support chatbot does not itself make booking or tenancy decisions.

Email info@akolet.co.ug with enough detail to locate the records and understand the request. We may need proportionate proof of identity or authority to act for someone else. Ask for a secure submission route for identity evidence. We must protect other people's information when providing a copy. Current PDPO forms, including Form 8 for access, are available through the Personal Data Protection Office; our team should help you use the appropriate procedure.

We will give the access decision within seven days and comply promptly within the Act's 30-day outer period, subject to its conditions. We will give the written decision on correction or deletion within seven days. Notices concerning prevention of processing or direct marketing have applicable 14-day response requirements. Where the automated-decision rules apply, we must explain and follow the relevant notification and reconsideration procedure, including its 21-day periods; the implementing regulations also contain a 14-day response path. If we refuse or partly refuse a request, we will explain the reason and complaint route. These legal timelines must be supported by an operational request process before publication.

## 13 Complaints and changes

You can contact our privacy lead and complain directly to Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office at https://pdpo.go.ug. You do not have to exhaust our internal process before exercising a statutory complaint or court right. Use the current regulator portal for complaint forms and official contact details.

We will date and version this policy, keep prior versions and communicate material changes in a suitable way. A changed notice does not retrospectively authorise a new use or replace consent where consent is required. The CampusHomes Terms and Conditions explain the service relationship; they do not remove your statutory privacy rights.
